App Guide
Send Feedback
Game Library
  • Auto-Scan: Baddel automatically detects all your installed games from Steam, Epic, EA, Ubisoft, Riot, Rockstar, and Discord.
  • Two Views: Switch between All Games (everything you own) and Installed Games (ready to play).
  • Manual Add: Use the "+ Add Game" button to add any game not detected automatically.
Search, Filter & Sort
  • Search: Type in the search bar to instantly filter your library.
  • Filters: Filter by platform, playtime, last played, and more.
  • Grid / List: Toggle between card grid view and compact list view.
Surprise Me
  • Play Mode: Picks a random game from your installed games and launches it.
  • Install Mode: Picks a random game you own but haven't installed yet.
  • Custom Pool: Set a specific list of games for the roulette to pick from.
Creator Mode
  • Customize Any Game: Change hero image, poster, logo, description, screenshots, and trailers.
  • Drag & Drop: Drop images or videos directly onto a game's page to update it.
  • Export / Import: Share your custom page packs with friends.
Synced Library
  • Connect Accounts: Link your Steam and Epic Games accounts.
  • Uninstalled Games: See all games you own across platforms that aren't installed yet.
Collections
  • Custom Collections: Organize your games into unlimited groups.
  • Favorites: Star any game to add it to your Favorites list.
  • Reorder: Drag and drop games within any collection.
Playtime & Stats
  • Auto-Tracking: Baddel tracks how long you play each game automatically.
  • Lifetime Stats: See your total playtime across your entire library.
  • Per-Game Toggle: Disable tracking for any specific game.
System Monitor
  • Live CPU, GPU, RAM, and Network usage shown while you browse your library.
